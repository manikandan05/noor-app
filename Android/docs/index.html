Noor – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Noor ("the app", "we") is an Islamic calendar, prayer times and daily wisdom app. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves your settings (language, theme, colour palette, reminder choices, prayer-time adjustments, and saved items) in your device's local storage. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

2. Location

If you allow it, the app reads your approximate location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. The calculation happens on your device. We do not collect, store or transmit your location to any server of ours. You can deny or revoke the permission at any time in your device settings, and enter times manually or adjust them instead.

3. Notifications and alarms

The app can send reminders and prayer-time alerts. These are scheduled locally on your device. You can turn them off in the app or in system settings.

4. Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and other device and usage information to serve and measure ads, and, where you consent, to personalise them. This is handled by Google under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy. Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other regions are shown a consent form before ads are requested. You can change your choice later where the form allows it, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

5. Premium subscription

Noor offers an optional Premium subscription (monthly or yearly, sometimes with a free trial) that removes ads and unlocks extra reading voices and colour themes. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or other payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account has an active subscription, and keeps a simple on/off flag with the time it was last checked in your device's local storage. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel; you can cancel at any time, including during a free trial, in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. After you cancel, Premium stays active until the end of the period you paid for (or the end of the trial). Refunds are handled by Google Play under its own terms.

6. Read Aloud (text to speech)

The Read Aloud feature uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone. The text is spoken by that engine, which is provided by your device or its provider (for example Google) and is governed by that provider's own privacy policy. Some engine voices may need an internet connection and are then processed by the engine's provider. We do not receive or store this text or audio.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or collect your name, email or phone number.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 8, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data retention and deletion

Because the app's data is stored only on your device, you can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will change when we do.
